About PixSort

Your camera roll, easier to use.

PixSort is an iPhone photo organizer that turns a crowded camera roll into useful albums you can review and keep in Apple Photos.

What PixSort does

Organizes photos and videos

PixSort groups selected library items into albums so you can find moments and clips without sorting everything by hand.

Puts you in control of cleanup

PixSort can help surface clutter, but you decide what to remove. The app does not delete photos without your approval.

Who uses it

PixSort is for people with large iPhone photo libraries, including creators looking for clips and families trying to make everyday photos easier to find.

How it works

  1. Choose the photos PixSort can access.
  2. Give the separate in-app permission before thumbnails and metadata are sent for AI sorting.
  3. Review the resulting albums and choose which changes to keep in your photo library.

When you enable AI sorting, selected photo analysis images are processed through Astralab Studios' hosting provider Render and Google Gemini. Read the privacy policy for details about photo and face data.

The company behind PixSort

PixSort is operated by Astralab Studios, Inc. For product, privacy, or account questions, contact support@astralabstudios.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do my photos always stay on my phone?

No. If you allow AI sorting, selected thumbnails, metadata, and photo analysis images are sent to the service providers described above. PixSort asks for permission before that processing.

Does PixSort delete photos for me?

No. The cleanup feature presents choices, and you approve any deletion.

Where can I get help?

Email support@astralabstudios.com with the app name and a description of the issue.
