What is PixSort?
PixSort is an iPhone app that uses AI to auto-organize your camera roll into smart albums by subject, event, person, or any custom category you create. Then it pushes those albums into native iOS Photos so you can hand them to your editor in seconds.
Does it work on videos too?
Yes. PixSort organizes both photos and videos. It's built specifically for people who shoot a lot of business content and need their team to find specific clips fast.
Will it delete my photos?
Never. PixSort only organizes. It never deletes anything without your explicit approval. The Clean Up feature shows you duplicates and screenshots, and you decide what goes.
