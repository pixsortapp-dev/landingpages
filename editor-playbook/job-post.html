How to filter

The paid test edit is the whole game.

  1. Skim portfolios first. Skip anyone whose vertical reel work doesn't match your aesthetic.
  2. Read their cover note. Generic AI copy-paste → pass. Someone who references YOUR content specifically → flag.
  3. Shortlist 5–10 people max. Don't over-screen on paper. The work tells you who's real.
  4. Pay each $20 for ONE test video from a real brief. Same brief for everyone — apples to apples.
  5. Hire the 1–2 who nail the test on the first try. If they need 3 rounds of edits, they're not it.

The paid test edit is the single most important filter. Don't skip it.
